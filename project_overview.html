normalization

Raw vs normalized CER

Five standard Arabic normalization steps (alef, taa marbuta, diacritics, kashida, punctuation) applied to both reference and hypothesis before scoring. The zero-shot model benefits most — 21% of its apparent error was orthographic convention mismatch, not misreading.

Qwen + LoRA (checkpoint-1120) 0.3283 → 0.3043  ↓ 7.3%
Qwen zero-shot 0.4442 → 0.3506  ↓ 21.1%
EasyOCR (10 samples) 0.4377 → 0.4007  ↓ 8.5%

Gray bar = raw CER · Teal bar = normalized CER · Max bar width = CER 0.50
